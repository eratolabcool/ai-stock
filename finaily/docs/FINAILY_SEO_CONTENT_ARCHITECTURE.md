# Finaily SEO Content Architecture

## Goal
Build a scalable AI investing intelligence content system for finaily.net.

## Content Layers

### Stock Pages
- /stocks/[symbol]
- Company overview
- AI exposure
- Finaily score
- Research summary

### Theme Pages
- /themes/[slug]
- AI sectors
- Industry maps
- Related companies

### Guides
- /guides/[slug]
- Educational investing content
- Long-tail SEO acquisition

### Reports
- /reports/[slug]
- AI generated market intelligence

## Generation Pipeline

Stock data -> Research Engine -> AI Summary -> SEO Content -> Database -> Next.js Pages

## Quality Requirements

- Avoid thin pages
- Add unique research signals
- Include risk factors
- Maintain financial disclaimers
